## Notes per still

**3.** Pulse rifle + underslung launcher is *Aliens*, not 1979 *Alien*. Issue #9 is right.

**5.** “RIPLEY” on the locker and the quilted suit are the *Narcissus* ending of *Alien* (1979), not TMP.

**9.** Green grass and trees argue against *Duel in the Sun*’s desert climax. Matches the Spartacus outdoor kiss. Not quite CONFIRMED without a face lock on Kirk Douglas / Jean Simmons.

**13.** Not a famous Ray Liotta / Pesci *Goodfellas* composition. Fits the *Léon* **apartment** (Mathilda’s father / waiting-on-the-deal), which is what issue #9 named. Face is not Jean Reno; keep PROBABLE.

**14.** Court masquerade with handled gold masks and a Louis XIV wig is *The Man in the Iron Mask*, not Kubrick’s *Eyes Wide Shut* mansion.

**16.** Opening of *Les Visiteurs*: Godefroy beheads the giant English knight; the body stays standing. That is not *The 13th Warrior*.

**23.** Nova Corps briefing on Xandar. Not *Valerian*.

**24.** Period McDonald’s night exterior is in the Muncie stretch of *Close Encounters*. *Ordinary People* has no such shot. A 1970s McDonald’s also appears in other films (e.g. *Zodiac*); CE3K is still the better fit with issue #9. PROBABLE.

**27.** T-800 biker-bar boots, not *The Lost Boys*.
