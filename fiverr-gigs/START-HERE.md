## Why these are “unique” (not another ChatGPT gig)

1. **YouTube Kids / COPPA-safe** — almost nobody writes this in the title.
2. **60-second timed beats** — not “a script”, a clock: 0–3s hook, 3–12s character, etc.
3. **Same cartoon character lock** — buyers want a channel that looks like one show, not 10 random animals.
4. **Free-tool version** — Bing pictures + CapCut. Buyers who refuse subscriptions will pick you.
5. **CapCut shot list** — seconds + zoom/pan per image. That is the product. Scripts alone are cheap.
6. **Parent-safe rules** — no jump scares, no “don’t tell grown-ups”, no fake kid voice tricks.

## How you actually get the first order

1. Open 1 gig (Kids Shorts pack).
2. Make 3 gig pictures (phone screenshots of a sample pack — see `sample-pack/`).
3. Share the gig in **your own** Facebook/WhatsApp “moms / teachers / YouTube newbies” groups. Do not spam Fiverr community with “check my gig”.
4. First 3 orders: over-deliver (11 scripts if they paid for 10). Ask for a review.
5. Do not drop to $5. Fiverr takes 20%. $5 = $4 and bad clients.

## Do not sell (oversold or risky)

- “I will build any app / SaaS”
- Generic “ChatGPT article 500 words”
- Homework / essays / exams
- Fake engagement, fake reviews
- Logo “design” if you only type a prompt and send 1 image
- Adult / dating / crypto pump content
