## SHORT 1 — Why do we wash our hands? (58s)

**Title options**
1. Pip sneezed on his sandwich (wash hands)
2. Tiny hitchhikers! Hand washing for kids
3. Soap is a bus for germs

**Spoken script (read this)**

(0–3s) Pip just sneezed on his sandwich. Uh-oh.
(3–12s) Hi, I’m Pip. Germs are tiny hitchhikers. We can’t see them, but they love sticky hands.
(12–45s) First, water on. Then soap. Rub palms. Rub the backs. Rub between fingers. Count to twenty — that’s one little song. Rinse. Dry.
(45–55s) Remember: soap is the bus that takes germs away.
(55–58s) Come back tomorrow. Pip’s packing a yellow backpack again!

**Captions (big, 3–5 words at a time)**
UH-OH, SANDWICH! → GERMS ARE HITCHHIKERS → WATER + SOAP → RUB AND COUNT 20 → SOAP IS THE BUS → SEE YOU TOMORROW

**Bing prompts (add character lock in front of each)**
1. Pip looks shocked at a cartoon sandwich, kitchen, medium shot
2. Pip waves at camera, big smile, classroom pastel
3. Tiny cute cartoon germs as round hitchhikers with backpacks, not scary, sitting on a paw
4. Pip turns on a tap, water sparkles, happy
5. Pip rubs soap between paws, foam bubbles
6. Pip counts on paws, soap bubbles in air
7. Germs hop onto a smiling soap ‘bus’, playful
8. Pip shows clean paws, thumbs up, sparkles

**CapCut**
Images 1–8, 6–7 seconds each. Animation: Zoom in, then Pan left on image 3. Voice over full script. Music: YouTube Audio Library, kids/ukulele, volume 10%.

## SHORT 2 — Why is the sky blue? (59s)

**Title:** Pip asks: why is the sky blue?

**Spoken**

(0–3s) Pip, why is the sky blueberry-colored?
(3–12s) Not paint. Not blueberry juice. Sunlight is a mix of colors, like a quiet rainbow.
(12–45s) Air is full of tiny bits. Blue light bounces around more than the other colors. So the sky looks blue to our eyes. At sunset, the light travels farther and we see orange and pink.
(45–55s) Remember: blue light bounces. That’s the sky’s trick.
(55–59s) Subscribe for more Pip questions!

**Captions**
WHY BLUE? → SUNLIGHT IS A MIX → BLUE BOUNCES → SUNSET = PINK → BLUE LIGHT BOUNCES

**Prompts (after character lock)**
1. Pip points up at a huge pastel sky, park
2. Pip holds a small cartoon rainbow, curious
3. Tiny sparkles bouncing in blue air, Pip watching
4. Same park at sunset, orange-pink sky, Pip sits on grass
5. Pip nods, points at camera, smile

**CapCut:** 5 images, ~10s, slow zoom. Do not use scary storms.

## SHORT 3 — 1 to 5 with apples (57s)

**Title:** Count 1 to 5 with Pip

**Spoken**

(0–3s) How many apples can a fox count?
(3–12s) Let’s go slow. One apple. Just one.
(12–45s) Two apples. Three. Four. Five! We can clap five times. One, two, three, four, five.
(45–55s) You counted to five. That is real math.
(55–57s) Tomorrow we count to ten.

**Captions**
HOW MANY? → 1 APPLE → 2… 3… 4… 5! → YOU DID MATH

**Prompts**
1. Pip holds 1 red apple, white background pastel
2. Two apples beside Pip
3. Three apples in a row
4. Four apples, Pip jumping
5. Five apples, Pip claps, confetti dots (not messy)

**CapCut:** hard cuts on each number. Optional on-screen big “1” “2” “3” added in CapCut (not in the AI image).
