### Title
I will write kids YouTube Shorts scripts with cartoon prompts

(or Video & Animation → Video Gigs → Video Advice if that category is open)

kids youtube shorts, educational video script, faceless youtube, capcut script, cartoon video prompts

1. “10 Kids Shorts. 60 seconds. Ready for CapCut.”
2. “Same cartoon character every video (not random animals)”
3. “Parent-safe + YouTube Kids wording. Free Bing + CapCut method.”

### Opening line (first 2 lines buyers see)
Stop posting random cartoons. I write 60-second kids education Shorts with a locked character, timed beats, and free CapCut steps so you can publish the same day.

### Full description

Need a kids channel that looks like a show, not a pile of AI clips?

I write Shorts made for ages 3–8:

• 60-second timing (hook, lesson, recap, subscribe)
• One cartoon mascot you reuse every episode
• Spoken words + big caption lines
• Picture prompts for Bing Image Creator (no paid video app)
• CapCut cut list: each image, how many seconds, zoom or pan

YouTube Kids / parent-safe:
No jump scares, no “don’t tell your parents”, no hard selling, no realistic child faces.

How it works
1. You send age, topic, and mascot (or I pick one).
2. I send a packed PDF/Doc you can copy into CapCut and Bing.
3. You generate pictures, drop them on the timeline, add free voice, export.

This is for new channels that want to post daily without Kling/Runway subscriptions.

### Packages

**Basic — $20 — 2 days**  
3 Shorts (one topic series). Scripts + captions + 8–10 image prompts each + CapCut seconds.

**Standard — $45 — 3 days**  
10 Shorts. One locked character bible. Thumbnail titles. 10 extra prompts.

**Premium — $79 — 5 days**  
20 Shorts + 30-day posting calendar + 3 series names + 20 thumbnail titles.

**Extras**
- Rush 24h: +$15 (Basic only)
- Extra character (2nd mascot): +$12
- Second language voiceover script: +$10 per 3 Shorts
- Thumbnail prompt pack (10): +$8

### Requirements (buyer form)
1. Age: 3–5 or 5–8
2. Topics you want (or “you choose”)
3. Mascot (animal + name) or “create one”
4. Channel vibe: calm teacher / silly friend / explorer
5. Language of the spoken script
6. Any words to avoid (religion, food, etc.)

### FAQ
**Do you make the actual MP4?**  
No. I write the pack. You make pictures in Bing (free) and edit in CapCut (free). Steps are included.

**Will the character look the same every time?**  
I give you a “character lock” paragraph to paste into every image prompt. That is how you keep one fox/robot/bear.

**Is this okay for YouTube Kids?**  
Scripts are written parent-safe. You still must mark “made for kids” yourself in YouTube Studio.

### Gig metadata
- Commercial use: Yes
- Revisions: 1 (Basic), 2 (Standard), 3 (Premium)

## Titles that get clicks vs titles that die

**Use**
- Number + outcome: “10 kids Shorts ready for CapCut”
- Pain: “same cartoon every video”
- Safety: “YouTube Kids safe”
- Time: “60-second”

**Avoid**
- “I will use ChatGPT / Cursor / AI” (buyers think junk)
- “Any script you want”
- “Viral guaranteed”
- All caps and 20 emojis
